BEFORE THE FIRST HELLO

A few things
you might be wondering.

Still have a question? Bring it to the first conversation.

Ask us anything ↗
What if we only have a few ideas?+

That's a lovely place to start. Share the occasion, a possible date and the atmosphere you have in mind. You do not need a finished plan before getting in touch.

Can you help with styling only?+

Yes. Tell us which parts are already organised and where you'd appreciate a creative eye, from a visual direction to the finishing touches.

What about smaller celebrations?+

Intimate occasions deserve thoughtful care too. Share your guest numbers, location and ideas so the support can be shaped around your gathering.

Where do you plan events?+

This concept focuses on Brisbane, the Gold Coast, Sunshine Coast and hinterland. Share your preferred venue or area with your enquiry.

How much support will we need?+

That depends on your occasion and what's already in place. Start with the priorities, guest numbers and an approximate budget, and we can work through the scope together.
