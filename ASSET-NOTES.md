See `assets/images/manifest.json` for measured dimensions, file hashes and generation prompts. Native PNG masters are encoded to lossless WebP without enlargement, colour overlays, filters or cropping. Responsive high-quality WebP derivatives are supplied at widths below each master’s native resolution; measured dimensions, byte sizes and PSNR values are in the manifest. The native lossless masters remain intact. Photographs are not repeated across sections. Gallery enlargement reuses its selected original, as expected for a viewer.

Outfit and DM Sans are self-hosted variable fonts from the Google Fonts repository, distributed with their SIL Open Font Licenses. The favicon is an original SVG mark. Decorative stars, rules and shapes are rendered with code.

All people and event scenes are illustrative generated content. They are not presented as real staff, clients or completed events.

The additional table-detail image was created with the built-in image generation tool. Its complete prompt is included in the manifest. It is a distinct new photograph, not a crop of the hero.
